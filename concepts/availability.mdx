---
title: "Availability and test mode"
description: "Understand which operations are contracts, fixtures, or live capabilities."
---

The OpenAPI reference and MCP tool list describe the interface. They do not guarantee that an account can run every operation.

As checked on September 22, 2026, the [published agent skill](https://gmapscrawl.com/skills/google-maps-data/SKILL.md) says paid API/MCP new-work operations remain disabled and test-key fixtures are supported. Start integrations with test mode and confirm live access with support before depending on production scraping.

Standalone review and photo jobs are not currently supported for live use. Business search can include available reviews and photos in its normalized business records. The review and photo tools remain in the contract for test fixtures and compatible clients.

| Response | Meaning | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simulated usage or `simulated: true` | Fixture data, not a live observation | Label it simulated |
| `capability_unavailable` | The requested capability is unavailable | Stop; check availability with support |
| `forbidden` | Account, plan, or scope does not permit the request | Check your key and entitlement |
| Accepted job | Work may still be running | Poll status, then inspect published results |

[Contact support](https://gmapscrawl.com/contact) for current access. Do not retry an unavailable operation indefinitely or present fixtures as real business data.
