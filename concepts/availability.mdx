---
title: "Availability"
---

Live search depends on your API plan, credential permissions, source availability, and server capacity. A `capability_unavailable` or capacity error means that search could not be admitted. Do not treat tool discovery as proof that a live request succeeded.

Only business search is offered. Standalone reviews, photos, asynchronous API exports, and customer webhook endpoints are not part of the Search API. The separate Online scraper retains its dashboard jobs and exports.
